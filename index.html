Help

Contact

Email zigmas.marketing@gmail.com. Say which iPhone you have and what you were doing when the problem happened.

I bought the full game but it is locked again

Open Settings in the game and tap Restore a purchase. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with. The full game then unlocks on this device.

What does the free version include?

Up to 20 prisoners, the first wave of research, minimum and normal security prisoners, the first five grants, and every speed except the fastest. One purchase unlocks everything else. There are no ads and nothing else for sale.

Where are my saved prisons?

On your phone only. The game keeps your current prison, a few earlier copies, and the prisons you save yourself. Deleting the app deletes them, and they do not move to a new phone.

I want a refund

Apple handles payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

The game is slow or my phone gets warm

Large prisons work the phone hard. Play at normal speed, close other apps, and keep the phone out of direct sun.

Privacy policy

In effect from 12 November 2026.

Lockdown has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Almost everything stays on your phone. The one exception is the purchase of the full game.

What the game keeps on your phone

Your prisons, settings and whether you own the full game are stored on your device. They are never sent to us.

What we do not collect

  • Your name, email, phone number or address
  • Your location, contacts, photos or microphone
  • How you play, crash reports or usage statistics

Children

Lockdown is not aimed at young children. We do not knowingly collect anything from anyone under 13.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove everything stored on your phone. The purchase record held by RevenueCat holds no name or contact details. To have it removed, email zigmas.marketing@gmail.com with the date of your purchase; you may then need to restore the purchase again.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
